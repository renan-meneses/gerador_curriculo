# Senior Software Engineer - Platform Team

**Company:** Tech Corp
**Location:** San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)
**Employment Type:** Full-time

## Job Description

We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Platform team. You will design, build, and maintain the infrastructure and services that power our product. You will work closely with product engineering teams to ensure reliable, scalable, and performant systems.

## Responsibilities

- Design and implement scalable distributed systems
- Build and maintain RESTful and gRPC APIs
- Optimize database performance and data pipelines
- Participate in on-call rotation for production systems
- Mentor junior engineers and conduct code reviews
- Contribute to architectural decisions and technical roadmap

## Required Qualifications

- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python or Go
- Experience with PostgreSQL or similar relational databases
- Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Experience with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Understanding of distributed systems and microservices
- Strong communication and collaboration skills

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with FastAPI or similar Python web frameworks
- Experience with event-driven architectures and message queues
- Knowledge of observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry)
- Experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform, Pulumi)
- Contributions to open-source projects
- Experience mentoring other engineers

## Technical Requirements

- Python, Go, TypeScript
- PostgreSQL, Redis
- Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform
- AWS or GCP
- REST, gRPC, GraphQL
- CI/CD (GitHub Actions, ArgoCD)

## Behavioral Competencies

- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
- Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment
- Proactive communication and ownership mentality
- Commitment to code quality and best practices
