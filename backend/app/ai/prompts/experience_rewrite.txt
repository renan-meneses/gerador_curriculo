OBJECTIVE:
Rewrite work experience bullet points to be more impactful, quantified, and ATS-friendly while preserving factual accuracy.

INPUT:
EXPERIENCE:
{experience_data}

TARGET JOB (optional):
{job_data}

OUTPUT SCHEMA:
- rewritten_bullets: [{original: string, rewritten: string, reason: string, confidence: number}]
- warnings: string[]

CONSTRAINTS:
1. Only use information explicitly present in the original experience data.

- Do not add metrics, percentages, or numbers not in the original.
